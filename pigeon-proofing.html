Pigeon proofing for solar panels in Perth

Pigeons love the warm, dry gap under solar panels. We clear out the nests and the mess, then fit mesh around the edge of your panels so the birds can't get back in.

Is it pigeons?

Signs birds are living under your panels

  • Cooing and scratching on the roof, often early in the morning.
  • Droppings on your panels, roof, gutters, windowsills or driveway.
  • Twigs and feathers poking out from under the edge of the panels.
  • Blocked gutters full of nest material and droppings.
Why it matters

What pigeons do to a solar system

  • Less power droppings on the glass block sunlight, so dirty panels make less power.
  • Mess and stains droppings build up on your roof and in your gutters.
  • Damage to wiring nesting birds can damage the cables under your panels.
